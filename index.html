Privacy Policy for win-car

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Overview

win-car ("we", "our") operates an automated Facebook Messenger chatbot designed to provide users with real-time bus locations and schedule updates in Nabeul.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We respect your privacy. Our chatbot does not collect or store personal identifying information such as your full name, email address, phone number, or precise device location.

  • Facebook Page-Scoped ID (PSID): Temporarily processed by Meta Graph API solely to send automated response messages back to your chat.
  • User Queries: Text messages (e.g., "bus") are processed in real-time to trigger automated responses and are not permanently stored on our servers.

3. Third-Party Services

Our service integrates with the Meta Graph API (Facebook Messenger) to handle messaging, and cloud backend databases solely for retrieving bus schedule data.

4. Data Retention and Contact

We do not retain, sell, or share personal data with any third parties. If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us directly through our official Facebook Page.
